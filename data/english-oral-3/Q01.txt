Question Q01 — Daily routines and learning English
Describe your usual study day. Explain when and how you practise English, describe one difficulty you have, and say what you do to improve. Give at least one specific example. Suggested speaking time: 60–90 seconds.

REFERENCE ANSWER (illustrative, not mandatory)
On weekdays, I attend classes in the morning and review my notes in the afternoon. I practise English for twenty minutes after dinner. I listen to a short podcast and write down new words. My main difficulty is remembering vocabulary. To improve, I use flashcards and make my own sentences. For example, yesterday I learned the word deadline and used it in a sentence about my homework.
