Question Q02 — A memorable experience and problem solving
Tell us about a memorable trip, school event, or group activity. Say when and where it happened and who was with you. Describe a problem, explain how you dealt with it, and say what you learned. Suggested speaking time: 90–120 seconds.

REFERENCE ANSWER (illustrative, not mandatory)
Last semester, I joined a school charity event with four classmates. We planned to sell drinks in the school yard, but heavy rain started before the event. We asked a teacher for permission to move our table into the hall. I made a new sign while my friends carried the drinks inside. In the end, many students visited our table and we raised enough money to buy books. I learned that clear communication and a backup plan can help a team solve unexpected problems.
