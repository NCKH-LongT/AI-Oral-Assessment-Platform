Question Q03 — Online learning or classroom learning
Some students prefer online learning, while others prefer learning in a classroom. Which do you prefer? Give two reasons and one specific example. Mention one disadvantage of your preferred option and suggest a way to manage it. Suggested speaking time: 90–120 seconds.

REFERENCE ANSWER (illustrative, not mandatory)
I prefer learning in a classroom for two reasons. First, I can ask my teacher questions immediately. Second, working with classmates helps me stay motivated. For example, in my English class we practise conversations in pairs, and my partner helps me find words when I am stuck. One disadvantage is the time I spend travelling to school. I manage this by taking the bus with a friend and reviewing vocabulary during the journey. Online lessons can also be useful, but classroom learning suits me better.
