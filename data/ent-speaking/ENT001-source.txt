                        ENT001- Speaking Test Guidelines and Sample


                                         General Overview
   •   Students take the test individually.
   •   A student chooses a speaking topic randomly (using https://wheelofnames.com/ )
   •   Part 1: The examiner asks the student 5 questions from the list. (2 minutes)
   •   Part 2: The student describes a picture using the prompt questions. (2 minutes for
       preparation, 2-3 minutes for speaking)


                                        Instructions for Examiner
Starting the test
          “Good morning/afternoon. Welcome to the speaking test. There are two parts to
          this test. In the first part, I will ask you 5 questions. In Part 2, you will describe a
          picture based on some questions I will give you.”


Part 1: Introduction
   1. Introduce Part 1:
               “Let’s begin with Part 1. I’m going to ask you 5 questions.”
   2. Switch to part 2:
               “That’s the end of Part 1. Let’s move on to Part 2.”
Part 2: Picture description
          “Now, for part 2, I’m going to show you a picture and you need to describe it in
          2 to 3 minutes. You will have 2 minutes to prepare. I will also give you some
          questions to help you think about what to say.”


Ending the test
          “That is the end of the speaking test. Thank you.”

                             ENT001 - Speaking Test Sample


Topic: Activities

Part 1: Introduction (2-3 minutes)
Note to the examiner:

Do not give the student time to prepare. Ask the student to introduce himself. Additionally, remind
the student to answer using complete sentences.
   1. Where are you from?
   2. What are your favorite clothes to school?
   3. What time do you eat breakfast?
   4. What is your favorite leisure activity?
   5. What's your typical day like?

[PAGE BREAK]
Part 2: Picture description (2-min preparation, 2-min answer)
Note to the examiner:

Give the student 2 minutes to prepare his answer for this part. The student has a maximum of 2
minutes to talk about the pictures using the given questions as guides.




       1. Who are they?
       2. Where are they?
       3. What are they wearing?
       4. What are they doing?
