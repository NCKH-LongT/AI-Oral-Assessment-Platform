                ENT303 - Speaking Test Guidelines and Sample
General Overview

1. Each test paper includes two parts. Students take the test in pairs.
2. The test includes a role-play of a randomly chosen topic and a discussion with the
examiner.
3. Timing: 10 minutes per pair.

Detailed Description for Each Part
Part 1. Role-play (2 minutes to prepare, 3-4 minutes to perform)
Each student randomly selects a topic and acts out a conversation with their partner based
on the provided information.

Part 2. Discussion (2 minutes/student)
The examiner asks each student 1-2 questions individually.
·
                                 Instructions for Examiner
A. Starting the Test

1. Gather students in the testing room, explain the test structure:
“ Welcome to the speaking test. This test will last about 10 minutes, with two parts: a role-
play and a discussion.
In the first part, you will work together on a role-play. You will randomly choose a topic,
prepare for 2 minutes, and then perform the conversation for 3-4 minutes”
2. Check attendance to ensure all students are present.
3. Instruct students to wait quietly outside until called.
4. Call each pair into the room by name to start the test.

B. Begin the test

Part 1: Role-play (2 minutes to prepare, 3-4 minutes to perform)

1. Topic selection
        “Now you will randomly pick a topic. You have 2 minutes to prepare the
        conversation"
2. Role-play
        “You can now start your conversation. You have 3-4 minutes for the role-play.”
3. Transition to Part 2
        “Thank you. We’ll now move on to the second part of the test.”

Part 2: Discussion (2 minutes/student)

“Now, for Part 2, I’m going to ask each of you 1-2 questions related to (specify the topic).”

C. Ending the Test
“That is the end of the speaking test. Thank you for your participation.”

Unit 1

Part 1 - Role-play ( 2mins. preparation; 3-4 mins. to speak)

With your partner, make a conversation about etiquette in your country.

Your conversation should include:
          -   The ways people greet each other
          -   Some conversation topics they should and should not ask
          -   The ways people address people formally
          -   Some do’s and don’ts in table manners

Unit 1


Part 2: Discussion questions (no preparation time; 2 mins to speak)

   1. In Vietnam, what are some things people do to be polite?
   2. What are some good table manners in Vietnam?
   3. Do you think people should be polite? Why is being polite important?
   4. Is it appropriate to hug or kiss another person in greeting? Why or why not?
   5. How can having good manners help you become successful?
