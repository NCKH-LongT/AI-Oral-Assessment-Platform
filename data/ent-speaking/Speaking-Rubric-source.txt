Ideas (maximum 2 points)
0 - below 0.6 — Insufficient evidence: Has no ideas at all, or all or most ideas are off-topic.
0.6 - below 1 — Attempt made: Has few ideas but ideas are not organized. Some ideas are off-topic.
1 - below 1.6 — Meets standard: Presents relevant ideas but some may be inadequately developed or unclear, or some supporting ideas may lack focus.
1.6 - 2 — Exceeds standard: Presents a well-developed response with clear organization to the question with relevant, extended and supported ideas.

Pronunciation (maximum 2 points)
0 - below 0.6 — Insufficient evidence: Pronunciation makes it impossible to understand speech. Or speaks nothing.
0.6 - below 1 — Attempt made: Speech is a little hard to be understood due to lots of errors in pronunciation.
1 - below 1.6 — Meets standard: Speaks with a clear voice but still in L1 accent. Speech is understandable. Some errors in word pronunciation, stress and sentence intonation may be found.
1.6 - 2 — Exceeds standard: Speaks with a clear voice and near-native pronunciation, stress and intonation. L1 accent has minimal effects.

Grammar Range and Accuracy (maximum 2 points)
0 - below 0.6 — Insufficient evidence: Speaks nothing or cannot produce basic sentence forms, or produce basic sentence forms with a lot of grammar mistakes that make it hard to understand the ideas.
0.6 - below 1 — Attempt made: Uses only simple sentences with errors. Errors prevent understanding frequently.
1 - below 1.6 — Meets standard: Simple sentences are mostly accurate. Some complex sentences are used, but these are limited in range, and they often have noticeable mistakes.
1.6 - 2 — Exceeds standard: Uses a wide range of structures flexibly. The majority of sentences are error free. A few simple errors may still be present, but they have “little to no” effect on the clarity, comprehensibility, and accuracy of the sentences.

Vocabulary Range and Accuracy (maximum 2 points)
0 - below 0.6 — Insufficient evidence: Speaks nothing or only produces isolated words or memorized utterances, or uses little vocabulary related to the topic.
0.6 - below 1 — Attempt made: Manages to talk about the topic using mainly simple vocabulary. Shows difficulty choosing vocabulary to speak. There are frequent inappropriacies and errors in word choice.
1 - below 1.6 — Meets standard: Vocabulary use is sufficient to discuss topics at length. There may be some repetition and sometimes inappropriate word choices, but the overall meaning is clear.
1.6 - 2 — Exceeds standard: Uses a wide vocabulary resource readily and flexibly to convey precise meaning. Uses less common and idiomatic vocabulary skillfully, with occasional inaccuracies. Uses paraphrase effectively.

Fluency and coherence (maximum 2 points)
0 - below 0.6 — Insufficient evidence: Maintains a short speech with some simple words or sentences and lots of hesitation to think, or speaks nothing.
0.6 - below 1 — Attempt made: Speaks with noticeable effort. Pauses, word repetition, and self-correction often happen. Uses very few linking words.
1 - below 1.6 — Meets standard: Can speak for a while and shows a readiness to talk at length. Sometimes loses track due to pausing, repeating, or correcting. Uses linking words to connect ideas when speaking, but not always in the right way.
1.6 - 2 — Exceeds standard: Speaks fluently with no or occasional repetition or self-correction. Any pauses are just to plan what to say next, not to find the right words. Uses a wide range of appropriate linking words.
